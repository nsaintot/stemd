demucs pretrained weights

These weights are a conversion of a demucs pretrained model:
https://github.com/facebookresearch/demucs

The demucs code is MIT. The weights are not covered by that licence. The
demucs maintainer wrote: "The model weights are not covered by the MIT
license, and are provided only for scientific purposes."
https://github.com/facebookresearch/demucs/issues/327

In https://github.com/facebookresearch/demucs/issues/267 the maintainer wrote:
"One limitation is the license of the MusDB dataset, which means the
pre-trained weights are mostly usable for research purpose. Making a real app
of it is out of the scope of those terms, and so this is not something I can
officially support through Facebook."

https://github.com/nsaintot/stemd/blob/main/docs/models.md
